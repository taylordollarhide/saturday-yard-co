Weekly tip

Taylor's Colorado lawn routine

  • WaterEarly morning before heat hits. Calibrate for depth on restriction days, not duration.
  • MowKeep the deck high in hot weeks. Never scalp — stressed turf takes weeks to bounce back.
  • EdgeClean hard edges make the whole yard read as maintained, even between upgrades.
  • RecoverSpot seed bare areas early. Small patches are a one-day fix; ignored patches become a renovation.

From a homeowner in Erie, CO

"My front yard burns out every July no matter how much I water. What am I missing?"
Nine times out of ten it's sprinkler coverage, not schedule. Walk your yard while the system runs and look for dry gaps between heads. Fix coverage first — then dial in the timing.
